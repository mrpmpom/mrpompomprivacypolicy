Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Mr. Pom Pom Discord bot ("the bot") handles user data. By using the bot, you agree to the terms below.

1. What Data We Collect

Mr. Pom Pom collects only the minimal amount of data required to provide its core features. This may include:

  • User IDs (for moderation logs like warnings, mutes, bans)
  • Server IDs and channel IDs (for configuration)
  • Message content (only when moderation filters are active — not stored)

2. How We Use Data

The data collected is used strictly for:

  • Executing moderation commands (e.g., tracking warnings, kicks)
  • Handling verification status and role assignments
  • Improving bot functionality and safety

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We do not store message history or private content unless specifically required for moderation purposes.

4. Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Your data stays between your server and the bot.

5. User Rights

Server owners and users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the developer via Discord or email. Data will be removed within a reasonable time frame.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance of the most recent version.

7. Contact

For questions or concerns, contact the developer at vrzix.h or mrpmpom on discord.
